Who Kleo is

A young Lirien of the Ironbark Guard: disciplined, stubborn, the last guard at a post that keeps getting emptier. Her short cloak is her folded, dormant wings, drawn as cloth until the Wing-Cloak wakes them. Later in the game she wears a wide-brimmed hat and a long coat (mockup I); that look gets its own model sheet and is not used in the animation cards yet.

How to use this book

Make KL0 first and approve it.

The master model sheet is attached to every other card. If it is off, every animation will be off. After it, the pilot is KL1 (idle) and KL2 (run): the two you will see most.

  1. Attach the images each card lists. They are in docs/art-production/lostkeep-attach/. After KL0 is approved, attach it to every card as "your approved KL0".
  2. Press Copy and paste the prompt into your image model. The copy includes Kleo's description and the sheet rules unless you untick the box at the top.
  3. Check the result against the checklist. Flip through the frames: she must stay the same size and the same person.
  4. Save it under the card's file name and hand it to me. I reduce it to true pixel scale, snap it to the palette, line up the frames on the anchor and build the SpriteFrames in Godot.
  5. Tick it done. Ticks are saved in this browser only.

The motion standard

Every animation in this book is held to the best hand-animated heroes in the genre. Nothing basic, nothing generic. Pixel art only feels this good when every frame is a deliberate drawing, so the cards give each move more frames than usual and describe every one.

How it should feel

  • Anticipation, contact, follow-through: every action winds up, hits a sharp key pose, and carries through.
  • Holds and speed: hold the strongest pose; move fast between poses. The game adds hit-stop on contact.
  • Overlap: the ponytail and cloak lag behind her, overshoot and settle a frame or two later.
  • Weight: she squashes into landings, pushes off the ground, and her hips lead her shoulders.

Frame counts

  • Idle and run: 12 frames each, so they never look mechanical.
  • Each combo hit: 12 frames; the finisher 16.
  • Death: 16 frames. Ledge grab and pull-up: 12.
  • The game adds what pixels cannot: hit-stop, screen shake, dust, glow and light.

Sheet rules

The grid

  • Every sheet is 4 columns of 384 × 384 px cells, with 2, 3 or 4 rows (8, 12 or 16 frames) as each card states. Frames run left to right, top to bottom.
  • In the game each cell is 48 × 48 px (one art pixel is 8 × 8 px in the master).
  • Kleo faces right in every frame. Godot mirrors her for left.

The anchor

  • The point between her feet when standing is at x 192, y 344 in every cell (art pixel 24, 43).
  • Standing, she is 224 px tall (28 art pixels) from boot soles to the top of her head; the ponytail rises a little above.
  • In the air, her belt stays near y 264, where it is when standing, so she does not jump around the cell.
  • The sword, ponytail and cloak may swing out but stay 16 px inside every cell edge.

Drawn in, and added by the game

  • Drawn into the frames: the ponytail, the cloak, the sword, the lantern at her hip.
  • Added by the game: slash arcs, the Spark orb, the Lance beam, the parry flash, glows, dust (the FX cards).
  • Never painted on Kleo: halos, light falling on her, motion blur.

Motion

  • Strong silhouettes and clear key poses: readable at 48 px.
  • Anticipation before every strike, follow-through after it.
  • The ponytail and cloak lag behind the body and settle a frame later.

Godot setup

  1. Sheets go in res://assets/characters/kleo/. Import with Filter Nearest, Mipmaps off.
  2. Create kleo.tres (SpriteFrames). For each animation: Add Frames from Sprite Sheet, Horizontal 4, Vertical 2, 3 or 4 as the sheet has, select the cells from the table in order, then set the FPS and loop flag.
  3. The player has an AnimatedSprite2D with centered on and offset (0, −19), so the anchor sits on the player's origin; flip_h is on when she faces left.
  4. The collision shape is a capsule about 10 × 26 px, independent of the art. Effects are separate scenes spawned at named points (the sword tip, her hand, the lantern).

Check every result

Flip through it

  • Step through the cells in order. Her feet stay on the anchor in grounded frames, and she does not grow or shrink.
  • The motion reads at a glance: strong key poses, anticipation and follow-through.
  • Looping animations (idle, run, glide, swim) join their last frame to their first.

Same character

  • Put it beside KL0. Same hair, face, jerkin, cloak, boots and sword in every frame.
  • The ponytail is on the same side of her head in every frame.
  • She faces right everywhere unless the card says otherwise.

Pixel art

  • Hard square pixels on one even grid, no blur and no anti-aliasing.
  • Her silhouette is readable when the sheet is shrunk to one eighth.

No painted effects

  • No slash trails, glows, halos or motion lines on Kleo's sheets: those are the FX cards.
  • No background, ground or wall drawn in any cell.
