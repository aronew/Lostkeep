# Labyrinth mockups

Twenty zoomed-out mockups of large, interconnected levels, made by the creative
lead as guidance for how a sector hangs together. They are **guidance for the
large shape**, not plans to copy tile for tile.

What to take from them:

- A sector is a cross-section: stacked strata of floor, joined at their ends
  and through a tall central space (a well, a shaft, a sinkhole).
- Loops everywhere: nearly every gallery can be reached two ways.
- Built stone and raw cave side by side, each breaking into the other.
- Pockets off the main ways: vaults, shrines, hideouts, flooded ends.
- Light as a compass: a shaft of daylight or moonlight marks the centre.

Where they differ from `design/metroidvania-principles.md`, the principles win:
several mockups show ladders and rows of small platforms. In the game those
become drops, large islands, and a machine for the long climb.

First used for IB-02, the Old Crypt (`design/rooms/ironbark_02_old_crypt.md`).
