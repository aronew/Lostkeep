# Lost Keep — Ironbark islands and cliffs

Seven individual island PNGs, a 3072×3072 island overview, and a 3072×3072 cliff/ruin atlas. The list requested seven cutouts despite its six-island heading. The 3584 px island cannot fit at full scale on a 3072 px sheet; individual island canvases preserve all requested widths. The overview uses reduced scales and is for review.

Exports use exact 8×8 flat-colour blocks aligned to the canvas origin, binary alpha transparency, and no painted background. Island widths refer to the entire cutout canvas, including roots and foliage. Manifest rectangles identify cliff pieces.

These are generated drafts. Exact reference matching, five tones per material, collision-straight walking lines, full 128 px stair treads, edge-light distances, and entirely hand-placed pixel authorship are not certified. Some silhouettes, colour edges, and ruin details need artist cleanup. Nearest-neighbour normalization reduces source detail. Crystals are painted pale teal; no game-side light halos are included.
