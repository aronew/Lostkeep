## The brief

The creative lead's mockup `docs/mockups/old_crypt/old-crypt-first-section.jpg`:
*"this mock up is exactly how I want that first section of the Old Crypt to
look"*, then their kit for it, OC1 to OC8 (`art/source/ironbark/crypt_oc/`).

It is a new room between IB-01 and IB-02: the hall door of the Lantern
Clearing now opens into it, and IB-02 (the galleries and the Root Well) lies
on beyond its two west doors. IB-02 keeps its plum crypt kit for now.

## The moody pass (after the creative lead's moody mockup of this room)

The creative lead's mockup `docs/mockups/mood/shrine_hall_moody.webp`: "I like
that look instead of so bright ... the fog looks really cool", and its top
galleries have "more character" than our straight corridor.

- **The mood is the room's own now** (`_mood = true` in `_build_air()`;
  `_moody()` in `room_builder.gd`): a teal murk (`MOOD_MURK`), a haze over the
  backdrops, ground fog along every floor run behind Kleo and thinner in front
  of her feet, a low bank in the drops, a bank along the bottom of the view,
  the warm lights stronger and the cold doorway glows dimmer, a deeper
  vignette.
- **The galleries step.** The west gallery's ceiling rises a course in its
  middle (row 2, x 8 to 12) and its floor steps up toward the gap (row 7,
  x 14 to 18). The east gallery: its west end on row 8 under a ceiling on
  row 3, a bulkhead (ceiling row 4, x 26 to 28), then a step down into the
  tall bay with the arcade (floor row 9, ceiling row 3, x 29 to 37), then on
  under row 4 to the gap by the East door. The doors' rows are unchanged.
- **Tried and taken out:** single-course hangs of stone under the galleries.
  On a three-tile slab the skin drew each as a window frame with a black slot.
  The mockup's sagging undersides wait for CH1 (slab ends and undersides).
- **Validation:** `check_room.py ib05` passes everything but its small-step
  count (4, rule <= 2): the steps are the mockup's, on purpose. Coverage 100 %,
  no traps, all four exits. `mode=tour` (it now hops the bay's step and jumps
  the gap onto the west gallery's step) and `mode=upper` pass.
- Image: `docs/rooms/ironbark_05/moody_vs_mockup.png` (the mockup above, the
  room below).

### The galleries after the moody mockup

The creative lead: the top hallway "still doesn't match the mockup look"; then,
with CH7's strips over the floors and ceilings, "a little wild", and "the top
hallways in the mockup are straight ... the floor is all uneven too". So: the
walking lines and ceilings are the skin's straight bands, stepping where the
plan steps; a brick back wall (`sh_fill`, `GalleryBackWall`) stands behind the
galleries; and CH7's cave strips, cut just under their cap, fill each slab
under its walking line (their ragged undersides hanging into the hall) and,
upside down, rise over each ceiling's band into the dark (the creative lead,
after the strips came off: "you had it mostly right before"). The strips start
8 px from each line (`BAND`), so the skin's straight band keeps the line
(`GALLERY_FLOORS`, `GALLERY_CEILINGS`, `_galleries()` in `build_ib05.gd`). They
have no collision. Collision unchanged; tours and checker as before.

### CH9 on the galleries

The creative lead: "the floors ceiling looked better last time, do we just need
new floor tiles for that hallway?" Yes: Book 1's CH9, the cobble of CH7 with
perfectly straight lines. The generator's own export is in (a first one,
rebuilt onto the grid by another tool, read too dark and flat and was dropped).
`_ch9_galleries()`: a floor band on every gallery floor run, a ceiling band on
every ceiling run, the underside strip under each slab; the skin skips those
cells (`CH9_SKIP`). The bands are lifted 1.7x in the game (`CH9_LIFT`): the art
is painted about half as bright as the CY stone and the room's murk multiplies
it. Collision unchanged; `mode=tour` and `mode=upper` pass. Image:
`docs/rooms/ironbark_05/ch9_vs_mockup.png` (mockup, the CH7 version, CH9).

CH9's floor step now turns each gallery step (the creative lead: "put those
step pieces in"): its face and upper floor only, on the upper walking line,
mirrored for steps down and upside down for the ceiling's steps (x 8, 13, 26,
29, 38). The two-tile ceiling step over the East door's gap keeps the skin.
Image: `docs/rooms/ironbark_05/ch9_steps.png`.

## Step 1: the CH pieces through the rest of the room

- **Slabs and corbels:** the seven small plates and ledges (west island, nook,
  pit ledge, the three plates, the ledge under the east gap) are CH1's small
  slab (38 px, walking line on its row 8), alternately mirrored. The two wall
  ledges are CH2's corbel, its pier in the wall: the west ledge is now 3 tiles
  (x 3 to 5) and the east shelf 3 tiles (x 51 to 53), to match the art. The
  shrine island, the arch island and the pedestal keep their SH art.
- **The detail pass in CH pieces** (`_ch_detail = true`; `_ch_room_detail()` in
  `room_builder.gd`): CH4's moss, roots, chains and stone drips under ceilings,
  each only where it leaves about 40 px of headroom; CH4's small moss over
  floor ends; CH5's crystals, tufts and rubble on floors; CH8's mounds, drums
  and grass darkened in front of floors (the mid step); CH8's thin root arches
  in black, sparse and only on the room's lower floors (the near step: grass
  and ferns in black read as blobs, and in a gallery they stood in the way).
  `detail_skip = SKIP` so the CH9 bands' skip regions don't keep it off the
  galleries and the hall's ceiling.

  island like in the mockup"): warm light at both painted braziers and a small
  glow at the statue's orb, kept low (the mood pass boosts warm lights).
